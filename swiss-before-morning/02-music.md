# SCORE — "Before Morning"

**Working title:** *New Romance*
**Format:** instrumental, 44s cut to picture plus a 90s full version for the
brand's own channels.

## Generation prompt

> Instrumental contemporary European luxury score, 92 BPM, D minor gradually
> resolving to F major. Intimate felt piano, muted analog pulse, low cello, soft
> brushed percussion, distant airport ambience and restrained sub-bass. Begin
> sparse and nocturnal, introduce a controlled rhythmic pulse as the car leaves
> Zürich, then open into warm strings at sunrise. Sophisticated, understated and
> emotionally anticipatory. No vocals, trailer drums, EDM drop, corporate uplift,
> or sentimental piano clichés.

## Structure against picture

| Time | Shots | Score |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:09 | 1–2 | Felt piano alone. No pulse, no tempo. Sparse enough to hear the cabin. |
| 0:09–0:18 | 3–4 | Low cello enters underneath. Still no rhythm. **Drop to near-silence one frame before the match cut.** |
| 0:18–0:23 | 5 | Single sustained cello note holds under the reveal. Space. |
| 0:23–0:30 | 6 | **The film opens here.** Analog pulse enters on the second indicator tick. Brushed percussion follows. |
| 0:30–0:38 | 7 | Full pulse locked to the cut. Strings bloom on the Lake Lucerne reveal. |
| 0:38–0:44 | 8 | Resolve D minor → F major. Strings hold. Cut to black on the held note, do not fade. |

## Notes for the composer

- The pulse is a texture, not a beat. If it makes anyone nod along, it is too much.
- Silence before the match cut at 0:18 is doing more work than any cue in the
  piece. Protect it in the mix.
- The F major resolution should feel like relief, not triumph. She arrived early;
  she didn't win anything.
- Sound design and score share one bus. Tyres, the trunk closing and the engine
  start are percussion — cut the score around them, not over them.

## Sound design spine

No voice-over anywhere. Only two lines of dialogue, both in the aircraft (Shots 2
and 3), both mixed close and quiet as if overheard.

Everything else: cabin roar, porcelain, wool, a seatbelt chime, the door seal, the
straight-six start, indicators, tyres, tunnel reverb, one throttle swell, wind,
and a cooling engine ticking.
